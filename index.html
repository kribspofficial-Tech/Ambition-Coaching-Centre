About the Institution

AMBITION Spoken English Centre was established on 9 November 2015 with the aim of helping learners improve their English communication skills and develop the confidence to express themselves in everyday, academic and professional situations. the centre provides a learning environment that encourages participation, regular practice and personal development. Our approach combines spoken English practice with vocabulary development, grammar in context, pronunciation, conversations, public speaking, debates and other communication-based activities. We believe that every learner has the potential to improve. With consistent practice, appropriate guidance and opportunities to communicate, students can gradually become more confident, capable and independent learners.

Vision

To become a trusted and respected institution for English communication, personality development and confidence-building education, with opportunities to reach more learners through future branches.

Mission

  • To make English communication more accessible to learners.
  • To encourage students to speak without unnecessary fear or hesitation.
  • To develop confidence, presentation skills and effective communication.
  • To provide opportunities for public speaking, debates and group discussions.
  • To support continuous learning and personal growth

Our core Values

• Student-Centred Learning (Supporting learners at different levels)
• Integrity (Honest communication and responsible practices)
• Continuous Improvement (Learning through practice and feedback)
• Participation (Creating opportunities for every learner to contribute)
